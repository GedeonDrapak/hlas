HLAS - FIRST RUN
================

1. Drag Hlas.app onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open Hlas from Applications. macOS will say it "could not verify that this
   app is free of malware" and refuse to open it.

   This is expected. Hlas is not notarized by Apple, because notarization
   requires a paid Apple Developer Program membership. The source code is
   public: https://github.com/GedeonDrapak/hlas

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to the message about Hlas. Confirm with Touch ID.

   Terminal shortcut if you prefer:
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Hlas.app

4. Hlas will ask for two permissions on first launch:
   - Microphone     - to hear you
   - Accessibility  - for the Fn hotkey and to paste text at your cursor

5. Hold Fn, say something, release. The text lands at your cursor.

Hlas lives in the menu bar, not the Dock. Click the waveform icon for
Settings, History, and the onboarding tour.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 15 or later.
