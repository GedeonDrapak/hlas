Is Hlas the cheapest dictation app?

Not always. Hlas Cloud at €35 a year costs less than the yearly plans of Superwhisper (€76.90), Wispr Flow and Willow (€130.29) and Aqua Voice (€108.58). One-time licences of VoiceInk (€22.62) and Spokenly Local+ (€26.24) are cheaper, and Šeptem and local Hlas are free.

When is Superwhisper the better choice?

When you want a lifetime licence, an iOS app or modes with custom prompts. Hlas is smaller (4 MB), uses less memory when idle and has extra tuning for Czech and Slovak.

When is Wispr Flow the better choice?

When you need sync across devices and team features. Wispr Flow runs in the cloud; Hlas can also transcribe offline on your computer.

When is MacWhisper the better choice?

When you mostly transcribe long recordings and meetings. Hlas is built for dictating into the cursor and handles audio files up to 10 minutes.
