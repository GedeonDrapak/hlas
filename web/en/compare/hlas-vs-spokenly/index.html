Is Hlas cheaper than Spokenly?

For local dictation, yes: Hlas is free with no limit, Spokenly costs Local+ $29 once, Pro $12 a month ($144 a year). A one-time licence pays off if you want to pay only once. Hlas Cloud at €35 a year is for people who do not want to download a model or deal with API keys.

When is Spokenly the better pick?

Spokenly runs on Mac, Windows, Linux, iOS and Android and has a one-time Local+ licence for $29. If you also dictate on your phone, it covers more devices.

Why switch to Hlas?

Hlas is free locally with no one-time fee. Unlimited cloud is €35 a year against $144 a year for Spokenly Pro, about 73 % less.

Which languages does Hlas support?

Czech, Slovak and English get extra tuning (vocabulary, spoken symbols, line breaks). 60+ more languages run through Whisper large-v3 without extra tuning.
