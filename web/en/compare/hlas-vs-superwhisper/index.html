Questions

Is Hlas cheaper than Superwhisper?

Yes. Hlas Cloud is €35 a year without VAT, Superwhisper comes to €76.90 a year ($8.49 a month, $84.99 a year, $249.99 once). That is about 54 % less. Local transcription in Hlas is free with no limit on top.

When is Superwhisper the better pick?

Superwhisper offers a lifetime licence ($249.99), many modes and settings and a long track record on the Mac. If you want to pay once and enjoy tweaking modes, it is a strong pick.

Why switch to Hlas?

Hlas is tiny (4 MB, about 60 MB of RAM when idle) and local transcription is free with no limit. Unlimited cloud transcription without keys is €35 a year against $84.99 a year for Superwhisper Pro, about 54 % less. Hlas also runs on Windows.

Which languages does Hlas support?

Czech, Slovak and English get extra tuning (vocabulary, spoken symbols, line breaks). 60+ more languages run through Whisper large-v3 without extra tuning.
