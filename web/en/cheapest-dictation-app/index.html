What is the cheapest dictation app?

Fully free is local dictation: Hlas (Mac and Windows) transcribes with Whisper on your computer, with no limit and no account. The cheapest unlimited subscription without API keys is Hlas Cloud at €35 a year. The cheapest one-time licence is VoiceInk at $25.

Is there free speech to text for Mac and Windows?

Yes. Hlas is free for local use on Mac and Windows and works offline. Czech, Slovak and English get extra tuning; 60+ more languages run through Whisper large-v3.

How much does transcription with my own API key cost?

Groq charges about $0.04 per hour of audio for whisper-large-v3-turbo. An hour of dictation a day comes to roughly $1.20 a month. Hlas supports your own Groq or OpenAI key for free.

Why is Hlas Cloud so cheap?

Hlas is a small native app with no extra infrastructure: audio goes through our server on Cloudflare straight to Groq. We store nothing and do not pay for expensive custom models, so the price can stay low.
