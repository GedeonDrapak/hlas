Hlas / Press kit

Press kit

Hlas is the lightest dictation app for Mac and Windows that understands Czech: free and offline, or in the cloud for €35 a year with no keys.

Quotable lines

  • Hlas is a 4 MB dictation app for Mac and Windows that types where your cursor is, with extra tuning for Czech and Slovak.
  • Local mode is free and works offline. Hlas Cloud costs €35 a year, 54 to 73 % less than the yearly plans of Superwhisper, Wispr Flow or Willow.
  • Idle, Hlas uses about 60 MB of RAM; on Apple Silicon it transcribes 11 seconds of speech in 1.7 seconds.
