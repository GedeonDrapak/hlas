Hlas Privacy
Effective date: 6 August 2026

Hlas is a menu-bar dictation app. It has no account system, advertising, analytics, or Hlas-operated cloud service.

Audio
When Local is selected, speech audio is processed on your Mac by the downloaded Whisper model. Hlas does not upload it.

When Groq or OpenAI is selected, Hlas sends the recording directly to the provider you selected so it can be transcribed. The provider's terms and privacy policy apply to that processing. Hlas does not relay the recording through its own server.

Stored on your Mac
API keys are stored in your macOS Keychain. Dictation history is stored locally in Application Support/Hlas and can be cleared in Settings. Diagnostic logs exclude transcript contents.

Permissions
Microphone permission is used only to record speech while dictating. Accessibility permission is used for the Fn shortcut and to paste the resulting text into the focused application.
